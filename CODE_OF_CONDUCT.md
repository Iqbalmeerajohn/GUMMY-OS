# Code of Conduct

## The short version

Be decent. Assume good faith. Criticise code, not people.

## What that means here

**Good:** asking why something was built a certain way; disagreeing with a
design and saying what you would do instead; pointing out that documentation
is wrong; saying "I don't understand this" without embarrassment.

**Not good:** personal attacks, harassment, demeaning language, sustained
disruption, publishing someone's private information, or treating anyone as
less welcome because of who they are.

This is a small project. Most friction is a misunderstanding rather than
malice, and a short clarifying reply usually resolves it.

## Scope

Issues, pull requests, discussions, and anywhere someone is representing the
project.

## Reporting

Email the maintainer or open a
[private advisory](https://github.com/Iqbalmeerajohn/GUMMY-OS/security/advisories/new).
Reports stay private. The maintainer will respond and may edit, remove, or
block as needed — and will say what was done and why.

Adapted in spirit from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org),
kept short deliberately: a document nobody reads protects nobody.
